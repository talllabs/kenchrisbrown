Greetings.

My name is KC Brown. I like to tell stories, mostly via pictures and moving pictures. I write. I direct. I produce. I shoot. I edit. I do lots of stuff. But mostly I try to make things that help people and brands show how awesome they are. Sometimes it works, sometimes it doesn't.

I've been lucky enough to live and work all over the world and meet some wonderful people. I started in California on my way to Sarajevo, Bangkok, London, LA, Jackson Hole and New Zealand and have been fortunate to work with a bunch of great brands.
